How nations divide across the continents

Link width = the percentage of a country’s land area that falls within a given continent. Most countries sit wholly in one continent (a single 100% link); a handful — Russia, Turkey, Kazakhstan, Egypt, Indonesia — straddle two, producing the thin secondary strands that cross the diagram.

Hover any country or continent to isolate its flows — the thin cross-continent links become traceable. Hover a link for the exact share.

Source: Mondial database, encompasses relation (251 links, 246 countries, 6 continents). Ordering computed by affinity seriation → weighted barycentre → consistent link sort to minimise crossings.
